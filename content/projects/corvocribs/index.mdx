## Context

Corvo Cribs was built at BeaverHacks with friends from Oregon State University.

Finding off-campus housing in Corvallis is frustrating: listings are stale, details are inconsistent, and students spend too much time jumping between websites.

## Goal

Build a free student-focused tool that centralizes housing discovery and helps students evaluate listings faster.

## What We Focused On

- Cleaner search experience
- Better listing quality signals
- Faster access to practical details

## Project Experience

The 24-hour hackathon constraint pushed us to prioritize UX clarity and team execution speed.
